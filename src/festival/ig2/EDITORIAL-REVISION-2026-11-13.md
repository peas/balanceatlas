The review calls for concrete prompts: “Como está sua canoinha?”, “Como chega o primeiro pé?”, “Do pike ao tuck”. Questions are useful when they direct attention to an observable action. They are not mandatory: statements, invitations and short explanations should alternate. Avoid generic engagement questions and promises of rapid mastery. Distinguish straight handstand from pike; validate terminology and instructional narration with Gabriel.
