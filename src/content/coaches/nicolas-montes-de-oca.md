Guinness World Record holder for the longest one-arm handstand. Trained at Beijing Circus School, ESAC (Belgium), and Academy of Circus of Kyiv. Has performed with Cirque du Soleil, Les 7 doigts de la main, and Franco Dragone. Special jury prize at Cirque de Demain 2019. Co-founder of [Balance Notion](https://balancenotion.com/).
