Founder of the London School of Hand Balance (2012). Born in Mongolia, former elite gymnast (two-time national champion). Toured internationally with Circus Krone and Blackpool Tower Circus. Directs handstand and acrobatic technique education at the National Centre for Circus Arts since 2004.
