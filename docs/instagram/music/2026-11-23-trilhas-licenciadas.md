## Sources and rights routes compared

1. **Mixkit, chosen today:** [Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/) explicitly includes social media video posts, online marketing and commercial projects. [Terms](https://mixkit.co/terms/) apply. Keep audio incorporated into the piece; do not redistribute a standalone music library, claim authorship or register it in rights-management systems. The site itself acknowledges claims can occur, so do not promise zero automated claims.
2. **Meta Sound Collection:** useful candidate for Meta-only distribution. Distinguish it from ordinary popular-song selection. The [terms page](https://www.facebook.com/legal/FB_Sound_Collection_Terms) and [music guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/legal/music_guidelines) redirected to login in this audit; no specific track from that collection was verified in the account today. Do not assume songs selectable through an extension are cleared for all commercial uses.
3. **Pixabay:** [FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/) allows music within larger commercial creative works and explicitly warns some tracks are Content ID registered. [License summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) and [terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/) apply. Good alternative with per-track records, not a zero-claims guarantee. No specific Pixabay track chosen today.
4. **YouTube Audio Library:** [official help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882) distinguishes attribution-required Creative Commons tracks from standard Audio Library licensing. Check each track's terms before cross-platform reuse; availability in YouTube is not enough for an Instagram campaign. No specific track cleared here.
5. **Popular Brazilian catalogue / native Instagram:** keep BaianaSystem, Maz/Luedji Luna, Jorge Ben etc. as aesthetic references from `MUSICA.md`; exact recording, account availability, commercial scope and any ad use remain separate checks. No downloads of commercial recordings from YouTube.
